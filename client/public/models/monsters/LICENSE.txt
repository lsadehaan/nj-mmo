Monster models (Phase 10)

Gremlin.glb, Goblin.glb
  Procedurally-generated low-poly rigged biped GLBs with 7-joint skeleton and
  4 animation clips (Idle, Walk, Attack, Death).  Generated by
  scripts/gen-glb-assets.py using pygltflib.  CC0 / public domain.

  Clip map: PROCEDURAL_BIPED_CLIP_MAP in creature-manifest.ts.
  Game scale: Gremlin ×0.52 (~0.6 m), Goblin ×0.61 (~0.7 m).

  REPLACE-BEFORE-LAUNCH: Replace with dedicated mob meshes (KayKit Dungeon Pack,
  Quaternius monster packs, or AI-generated) for higher visual fidelity before
  public launch (AD-004).  The generator script is in scripts/gen-glb-assets.py.
